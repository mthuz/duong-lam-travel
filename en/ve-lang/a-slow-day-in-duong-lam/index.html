A slow day in Duong Lam

06/11/2026

Duong Lam rewards visitors who slow down. Here is a simple rhythm for one day.

Morning: the lanes

Arrive early, before the day-trippers. Start at the Mong Phu village gate and let the brick lanes lead you past laterite walls and old wooden houses.

Noon: a country lunch

Look for a family-run place serving local chicken, garden greens and the village’s own soybean sauce.

Afternoon: two kings

Visit the temple of Phung Hung and the mausoleum of Ngo Quyen, two national heroes born in Duong Lam.

Before you leave

  • Buy che lam rice cakes or peanut candy from a family that makes them.
  • Always ask before entering a house or photographing people.
