Make chè lam by hand

This chewy local sweet combines toasted glutinous rice flour, malt syrup, sugar, ginger and peanuts. Learn to stir, shape and cut it with local hosts, then try it with hot tea.

What to look forward to: Take home the sweets you make.

Cook with a local family

Help prepare and season a northern Vietnamese family meal. The dishes depend on the season and menu.

  • Spring rolls: prepare the filling, roll and fry them.
  • Braised fish: cook with traditional soybean sauce.
  • Seasonal vegetables: prepare and cook everyday greens.
  • Other family dishes: learn how the family seasons its food.

What to look forward to: Sit down to share what you cooked.

Cycle around Duong Lam

Ride past laterite walls, tiled roofs, village gates and the surrounding fields. Stop for photos or a chat along the way.

What to look forward to: Explore at your own pace.

Explore the old village

Visit Mong Phu gate and communal house, old timber homes, the Phung Hung temple and the Ngo Quyen mausoleum.

What to look forward to: Learn how Duong Lam became known as the land of two kings.
