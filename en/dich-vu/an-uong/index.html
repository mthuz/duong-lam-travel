Dining at the old house

The timber rooms, tiled roof and brick courtyard make a welcoming place to eat. The family cooks for solo visitors, families and tour groups, with room for up to 150 guests.

Dishes from the family table

Mia chicken with lime leaves

Golden-skinned chicken with tender, sweet meat, shredded lime leaves and lime-pepper salt.

Crispy roast pork belly

Golden crackling and tender pork belly, served with greens.

Fish braised in soybean sauce

Fish simmered with ginger and fermented sticky-rice soybean sauce, served with hot rice.

Fried pork spring rolls

Crisp rolls filled with pork, wood ear mushrooms and glass noodles, served with sweet-and-sour fish sauce.

Seasonal greens and soup

Seasonal stir-fries, salads and soups, with tofu or dried radish.

Menus for different tastes

The family serves traditional Vietnamese dishes to both Vietnamese and international guests. The choice and combination of dishes can be adapted to your group’s tastes and dietary needs.

  • Traditional Vietnamese meal: Share Mia chicken, roast pork, braised fish, spring rolls, tofu and seasonal greens.
  • Menu for international visitors: Try local dishes selected with your group’s tastes in mind.
  • Menu by request: Tell us your group size, vegetarian preferences and other needs when booking.

Good to know: Dishes vary with the season and available ingredients.
